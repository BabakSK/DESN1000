# DESN1000 · Water in soil: interactive examples

Three interactive teaching examples on the flow of water through soil.

**Use them here: https://babaksk.github.io/DESN1000/**

| Example | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Flow and effective stress](https://babaksk.github.io/DESN1000/effective-stress.html) | A seepage rig with standpipes and total pressure cells; raise the supply until the sand boils, then the same physics in an excavation inside sheet piles. |
| [Darcy's law](https://babaksk.github.io/DESN1000/darcy-law.html) | A permeameter with one soil or two layers, in series or in parallel; run constant-head and falling-head tests, log trials and fit *k*. |
| [Earth dam seepage and piezometers](https://babaksk.github.io/DESN1000/earth-dam.html) | A dam section solved live (Laplace's equation with a free surface); plant piezometers, drag the reservoir, add a core, a drain or a cut-off. |

Each page runs entirely in the browser, with no installation and no internet connection needed once it is open. Press F11 for full screen when projecting.
